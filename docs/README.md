# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

OctoAcme uses a customer-first, iterative project-management lifecycle with clear ownership and data-informed decisions. Teams validate the business need and measurable outcomes during initiation, then plan a prioritized backlog, acceptance criteria, estimates, milestones, dependencies, Definition of Done, and QA approach.

Execution and tracking use a project board, small pull requests, CI checks, reviews, testing, regular delivery rhythms, metrics, and blocker escalation. Teams maintain a risk register, monitor dependencies, communicate regularly with stakeholders, and escalate issues through the team, Project Manager, Product Lead, and Sponsor as needed.

Before release, teams verify acceptance criteria, CI and security checks, release notes, rollback planning, staging smoke tests, production deployment, and post-deployment verification. Retrospectives after sprints, releases, milestones, and incidents turn learnings into owned, time-bound improvement actions. Developers, Product Managers, Project Managers, QA/Testing contributors, and stakeholders have defined responsibilities throughout this lifecycle.
