# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX/UI Designer or Researcher

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers or Researchers shape user-centered solutions through research, interaction design, and usability validation.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and conduct user research, journey mapping, and usability validation
- Produce and maintain design artifacts that communicate intended user experiences
- Partner on accessible, usable acceptance criteria and identify experience risks

### Accountability and Touchpoints
- Accountable for the quality and evidence behind user-experience recommendations; Product Managers prioritize resulting trade-offs
- Contribute research findings, user journeys, prototypes, and design specifications during discovery, planning, implementation, and validation

### Collaboration
- Work with Product Managers on outcomes and acceptance criteria; with Project Managers on research activities and dependencies
- Partner with Developers on feasibility and implementation, QA/Testing on usability coverage, and Stakeholders on feedback

---

## Business Analyst or Requirements Lead

### Role Summary
Business Analysts or Requirements Leads translate stakeholder needs into clear, traceable requirements that teams can plan and validate.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit, analyze, and document business needs, assumptions, and requirements
- Define and refine acceptance criteria and maintain traceability for requirements decisions
- Identify requirement gaps, dependencies, and changes for the project plan

### Accountability and Touchpoints
- Accountable for requirement clarity and traceability; Product Managers decide prioritization and Stakeholders provide business approval
- Maintain requirements, acceptance criteria, decision records, and process flows from initiation through refinement and validation

### Collaboration
- Partner with Product Managers and Project Managers during initiation and planning
- Work with Developers on refinement and feasibility, QA/Testing on validation coverage, and Stakeholders to confirm needs and decisions

---

## Technical Lead or Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads or Architects guide technical design, non-functional requirements, integration decisions, and technical risk mitigation.

### Responsibilities
- Define or review technical designs, architecture, integration approaches, and non-functional requirements
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and trade-offs and recommend mitigations
- Support implementation quality through technical guidance and design reviews

### Accountability and Touchpoints
- Accountable for coherent technical direction and documented trade-offs; Developers own implementation and Product Managers own product priorities
- Contribute architecture and design records, technical risk assessments, and implementation guidance during planning, delivery, and release readiness

### Collaboration
- Work closely with Developers on design and implementation; with Product Managers and Project Managers on scope, trade-offs, risks, and dependencies
- Partner with QA/Testing on testability, Security or Privacy Partners on technical controls, Operations on operability, and Stakeholders on material technical decisions

---

## Release Manager or Deployment Coordinator

### Role Summary
Release Managers or Deployment Coordinators coordinate release readiness, deployment communication, and post-deployment follow-through.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate readiness checks, deployment windows, release notes, rollback plans, and stakeholder announcements
- Track release dependencies, decisions, and outstanding readiness risks
- Coordinate post-deployment verification and capture follow-up actions

### Accountability and Touchpoints
- Accountable for coordinating the release process and communicating its status; release approval and deployment execution remain with the appropriate accountable teams
- Maintain release checklists, deployment plans, release notes, and verification records during planning, release preparation, deployment, and follow-up

### Collaboration
- Coordinate with Project Managers on timing and risks, Developers on readiness and rollback, and QA/Testing on validation results
- Partner with Operations on deployment execution, Customer Support or Enablement Leads on readiness, Product Managers on scope, and Stakeholders on release communication

---

## Operations/Site Reliability or Platform Engineer

### Role Summary
Operations, Site Reliability, or Platform Engineers support reliable, observable, and operable services and delivery environments.

### Responsibilities
- Prepare and maintain environments, platform capabilities, observability, and operational documentation
- Assess reliability, capacity, and operational risks and support incident response
- Support deployment readiness and post-release monitoring

### Accountability and Touchpoints
- Accountable for operational readiness and platform reliability within their area; Developers remain accountable for their service changes
- Contribute runbooks, monitoring and alerting guidance, operational-readiness assessments, and incident records before release and during operations

### Collaboration
- Work with Developers on operability and observability, QA/Testing on environment and reliability validation, and Release Managers on deployment readiness
- Partner with Project Managers on operational risks, Product Managers on reliability trade-offs, Security or Privacy Partners on operational controls, and Stakeholders during incidents

---

## Security or Privacy Partner

### Role Summary
Security or Privacy Partners advise teams on security and privacy requirements, risks, and validation throughout delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Identify security and privacy requirements and facilitate threat or privacy-risk assessment
- Advise on safeguards, validation approaches, and risk treatment options
- Escalate material concerns through the appropriate security or privacy path

### Accountability and Touchpoints
- Accountable for providing security and privacy guidance and documenting identified risks; accountable business owners make risk-acceptance decisions
- Contribute threat models, risk assessments, requirements, and validation recommendations during planning, implementation, testing, and release readiness

### Collaboration
- Work with Product Managers on requirements and trade-offs, Developers and Technical Leads on controls, and QA/Testing on security validation
- Partner with Project Managers on risk tracking, Operations on operational safeguards, Release Managers on readiness, and Stakeholders on material risk decisions

---

## Customer Support or Enablement Lead

### Role Summary
Customer Support or Enablement Leads prepare customer-facing teams for change and channel customer feedback into product delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Assess customer impact and prepare support documentation, training, and enablement materials
- Coordinate support readiness and communicate known changes, limitations, and escalation paths
- Collect customer feedback after release and share actionable insights with the delivery team

### Accountability and Touchpoints
- Accountable for support and enablement readiness; Product Managers prioritize customer-feedback responses and Release Managers coordinate release timing
- Maintain support guidance, training materials, customer-impact assessments, and feedback summaries before and after releases

### Collaboration
- Work with Product Managers on customer impact and feedback, Project Managers on readiness activities, and Release Managers on communications
- Partner with Developers and QA/Testing on known behaviors and troubleshooting, Operations on incident communication, and Stakeholders on customer outcomes
